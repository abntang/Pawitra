# Pawitra — Digital Heritage Experience

## Project Overview

Pawitra is an immersive digital storytelling experience about
Gunung Penanggungan (Pawitra) and its role in Javanese civilization.

This project is not a tourism website, archive website, or encyclopedia.

The goal is to create a digital essay that explores how ancient
Javanese society understood the relationship between:

- Humans
- Nature
- Spirituality
- Architecture
- Memory

The website should feel like reading a historical manuscript,
walking through a digital museum, and experiencing a landscape
that still carries memories from the past.

---

# Core Philosophy

## "Membaca Gunung Suci Jawa"

Gunung Penanggungan is presented not only as a geological object,
but as a cultural landscape.

The mountain is:

- A sacred space in ancient cosmology
- A source of life through water
- A place where stones preserve memory
- A landscape shaped by human spirituality
- A heritage that continues to live today

# Chapter Details

## 01 — Pawitra: Gunung yang Menghubungkan Dunia

Main idea:

Ancient Javanese society did not see mountains only as
geographical formations.

Mountains were considered sacred spaces connecting humans
with higher realms.

Reference concept:

Tantu Pagelaran describes the mythological placement of
Mount Meru in Java as a way to stabilize the island.

Important:
Present this as cultural mythology and worldview,
not scientific geography.

Visual direction:

- Cinematic Gunung Penanggungan image
- Mountain silhouette
- Topographic contour lines
- Slow camera movement

Interaction:

As the user scrolls:
- Mountain gradually revealed
- Contour lines appear
- Text fades naturally

---

## 02 — Tirta: Air yang Turun Membawa Kehidupan

Main idea:

Water represents the meeting point between:

- Human necessity
- Ancient engineering
- Spiritual meaning

Focus:

Petirtaan Jolotundo.

Visual:

- Dark blue atmosphere
- Flowing water
- Jaladwara details
- Subtle ambient sound

Avoid making water only mystical.
Show the relationship between belief and practical civilization.

---

## 03 — Batu: Bahasa yang Tidak Bersuara

Main idea:

Stone preserves memory.

Archaeological remains are not only objects,
but traces of how ancient people expressed ideas.

Visual:

- Relief photography
- Ancient structures
- Stone texture

Interaction:

Hover reveals details,
but avoid museum tooltip style.

The interaction should feel like:
"discovering meaning"

not:
"reading database information".

---

## 04 — Manusia: Mereka yang Menjaga Jejak

Main idea:

Civilization survives because humans continue to care for it.

Two timelines:

Past:
- Religious communities
- Ascetics
- Sacred landscape

Present:
- Archaeologists
- Researchers
- Local communities
- Explorers

Visual:

A transition between past and present.

Message:

"Peradaban tidak hanya diwariskan,
tetapi dirawat."

---

## 05 — Warisan: Sesuatu yang Tetap Hidup

Main idea:

A kingdom can disappear,
but meaning can survive.

Closing message:

Majapahit is not only a political empire from the past.

Its legacy continues through:

- Architecture
- Language
- Culture
- Relationship with nature

Ending feeling:

Not a conclusion,
but a continuation.

# Design Direction

## Overall Feeling

Keywords:

- Quiet
- Sacred
- Timeless
- Editorial
- Cinematic
- Reflective


Avoid:

- Tourism website aesthetic
- Fantasy medieval style
- Excessive gold ornaments
- Ancient decorative fonts
- Overcrowded museum interface


Prefer:

- Modern editorial design
- Digital humanities aesthetic
- Large meaningful imagery
- Strong typography
- Calm composition

---

# Typography

Primary Font:

Plus Jakarta Sans / Geist

Use for:
- Body
- UI
- Explanations

Secondary Font:

Cormorant Garamond

Use only for:
- Chapter titles
- Large quotes

Avoid:
- Hard-to-read classical fonts
- Excessive serif text

Readability is more important than historical appearance.

---

# Layout Principle

Minimal does not mean empty.

Avoid excessive whitespace.

Use:

- Editorial grid
- Asymmetric composition
- Balanced text-image relationship
- Clear visual hierarchy

Do not create:

- Centered title everywhere
- Huge empty margins
- Decorative elements just to fill space


Whitespace must have purpose.

---

# Interaction Style

Preferred:

- Smooth scrolling
- GSAP style cinematic transitions
- Subtle parallax
- Fade and reveal
- Slow movement

Avoid:

- Excessive animation
- Flashy effects
- Unnecessary 3D

The user should feel:
"I am discovering a story."

Not:
"I am watching a tech demo."

---

# Technical Direction

Recommended:

React
Vite
Tailwind CSS
GSAP ScrollTrigger
Lenis Smooth Scroll

No dependency on Google Earth 3D.

The focus is storytelling, not geographical simulation.
